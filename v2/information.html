Information

Rules, game modes and the systems that make the server tick. Read this before your first fight.

BattleCraft is a Full PvP server: combat is open outside of spawn, land can be claimed, and the economy is run by the players. The three steps below are all you need to get started.

  1. Add play.foxcraft.net to your multiplayer list (Java Edition 1.8 or newer).
  2. Pick a class at spawn and claim your first house plot with /house.
  3. Join a clan or create your own, then queue for the next arena event.
Rules The short version: play fair.
  1. No cheating. Hacked clients, macros and x-ray give an unfair advantage and end in a permanent ban.
  2. No griefing inside claimed land. Outside claims, PvP is always allowed.
  3. Respect other players. Slurs, harassment and doxxing are not part of the game.
  4. No advertising or account trading in chat.
  5. Report rule breakers with /report. Every case is reviewed by staff.
PvP Where combat is enabled and how it works.

Combat is enabled everywhere outside the spawn safe zone. During events keep-inventory is off, so bring gear you can afford to lose.

/spawn has a 10 second channel that is interrupted by damage, and logging out during combat leaves your character in the world for 15 seconds.

Economy system Earn coins, open a shop, trade with players.

Coins come from PvP kills, mining, quests and player shops. Use /bal to check your balance and /shop to open the server shop.

Players can create their own storefront with /pshop. Prices are set by the community, not by staff.

House system A base that only you and your clan can enter.

Claim a plot with /house and build a base that only you and your clan can enter. Plots grow with your rank and can be decorated with furniture from the shop.

Houses are protected from explosions, fire and unauthorized players.
