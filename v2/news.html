SaleNovember 18, 2026

Season sale: 50% off every rank

Until December 15 every rank in the shop is half price. The discount is applied automatically at checkout, so there is no code to remember.

Ranks are permanent and carry over between seasons. If you have been waiting to unlock extra homes or the colored chat tag, this is the cheapest moment of the year.

SeasonNovember 8, 2026

New season: fresh map, new events

The world has been reset with a brand new map. Land claims, chests and clans were migrated automatically, and ranks, coins and purchased items are untouched.

This season adds two weekly events: a mining rush on Saturdays and a clan tournament on Sundays. Both hand out exclusive cosmetics and coins.

ArenaNovember 2, 2026

The new arena is open

Built over 600 blocks of netherrack and obsidian, the new arena supports 8v8 fights with a spectator balcony and a live scoreboard.

Queue with /arena join or wait for the automatic event every three hours. The first tournament starts this weekend.
