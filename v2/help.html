Frequently asked questions

How do I join the server?

Open Minecraft Java Edition, go to Multiplayer, click Add Server and paste the IP shown in the top bar of this page. Double-click the server to connect.

Which Minecraft versions are supported?

Java Edition 1.8 through the latest release. The newest version gives the best performance, and PvP timing is the same on every supported version.

I found a bug or a rule breaker. What now?

Use /report in game or open a ticket in the Discord support channel. Include screenshots, the player's name and the time it happened.

Can I appeal a ban?

Yes. Open a ticket in Discord with your username and the reason shown when you were banned. Every appeal is reviewed by a staff member who was not involved in the original decision.

Do ranks and coins reset between seasons?

No. Ranks, coins and purchased items stay on your account forever. Only the world and land claims are reset at the start of each season.
